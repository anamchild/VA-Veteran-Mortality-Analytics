# Data

## Dataset Overview

This project uses publicly available data from the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) Office of Mental Health and Suicide Prevention.

The dataset is taken from:

**National Veteran Suicide Prevention Annual Report: 2024 Data Appendix – All-Cause Mortality (2018–2023)**

The data provide national-level mortality statistics for U.S. Veterans over a six-year period (2018–2023). The project compares mortality patterns between:

- Recent Veterans Health Administration (VHA) Users
- Other Veterans



## Dataset Characteristics

- **Source:** U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs
- **Time Period:** 2018–2023
- **Data Type:** Aggregated population-level mortality data
- **Format:** Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)

The dataset contains annual mortality statistics and Years of Potential Life Lost (YPLL) across multiple causes of death.



## Variables Included

The dataset includes:

- Year
- Veteran Group
- Cause of Death
- Number of Deaths
- Crude Mortality Rate
- Age-Adjusted Mortality Rate
- Years of Potential Life Lost (YPLL)



## Data Preparation

The data were:

- Imported into Google Colab using Python.
- Cleaned and organised for analysis.
- Reviewed for consistency across all reporting years.
- Combined into a single analytical dataset.
- Prepared for descriptive statistics, comparative analysis, trend analysis, and data visualisation.



## Data Limitations

The dataset contains aggregated national statistics rather than individual patient records.

Therefore:

- Individual-level predictive modelling was not appropriate.
- Regression analysis was not the primary analytical approach.
- The project focused on descriptive analytics, comparative analysis, longitudinal trend analysis, and Years of Potential Life Lost (YPLL).
